# sv-01 · Series bible

## Format
- Vertical 1080×1920, 2–3 minutes (target 120–180 s), one narrator, captions burned in.
- Flat "riso ink" 2D illustration (sv-20 tokens): ink-navy outlines, halftone dots, paper grain,
  Be Vietnam Pro type. Animated in Remotion, every shot anchored to a word.
- Sound: voice Hải Đăng (northern male), the series theme music (scripts/theme.py) 15–18 dB under the voice, mixed by the sv-42 standard, room
  ambience per location, small foley.

## Narrator
"Tôi" — an ordinary resident who lives inside the story (a tenant, a neighbour, the guard, the
maintenance man). Calm, tired, precise, never laughs at his own jokes. Knows numbers and names. Often
turns out to be part of the cause at the end.

## Comedic engine
One absurd rule enters ordinary Vietnamese life (a building, a lane, a market, a ward office, a school)
and is treated with total seriousness. People adapt, exploit, bureaucratise it, until the system (ward
committee, meeting minutes, price lists, statistics) absorbs the absurdity. A fair twist recasts it; a
short button flips it once more.

### Premise test (all must be yes)
1. Can the rule be said in one sentence a child understands?
2. Is it visible — can we draw the rule working in one frame?
3. Does it have a cost, so people must scheme around it?
4. Can it reach a system (meeting, office, market, statistics) within 2 minutes?
5. Is there a twist that is fair and changes the meaning of the start?
6. Does it happen in places we can build as sets (sv-20 location bank)?
7. Is it original — not a story, line or character from another channel?

## World and motifs
- Places: chung cư, ngõ, chợ, phường, tổ dân phố, nhà văn hoá, quán nước, trường học, bến xe.
- People: bà tổ trưởng, ông bảo vệ, cán bộ phường, bà bán nước, thằng cu, ông cụ, cặp vợ chồng trẻ,
  anh shipper. Named with a place: "Bà Hạnh tầng năm", "chú Toàn số nhà mười hai".
- Recurring gags: official notices with absurd wording, meeting minutes, red stamps, price boards, queues,
  floor numbers, loudspeaker announcements.

## Tone limits
- Mocking systems and habits, never a real person, party, ethnicity, religion, disability or tragedy.
- No vulgarity, no sexual content beyond a hint ("ngoại tình" as a plot fact is the ceiling).
- No real brands, no real places beyond generic ones ("chung cư B5", "phường").

## No copying
The user studied channels such as "Rô Tới Chơi" and "In this town". Learn general techniques (narration
over flat illustration, deadpan escalation) but never their premises, lines, characters, visual identity
or endings. Every brief's "Not copying" section must name the closest reference you know and say how this
episode differs.
