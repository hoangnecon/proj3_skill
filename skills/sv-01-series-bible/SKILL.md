# sv-01 · Series bible

## Format
- Vertical 1080×1920, 2–3 minutes (house standard 125–170 s, 620–860 words at 5.25 ups), one narrator, captions burned in.
- Flat "riso ink" 2D illustration (`sv-20` tokens): ink-navy outlines, halftone dots, paper grain,
  Be Vietnam Pro type. Animated in Remotion, every shot anchored to a word.
- Sound: voice Hải Đăng (northern male), the series theme music (`scripts/theme.py`) 15–18 dB under the voice, mixed by the `sv-42` standard, room ambience per location, small foley.

## Narrator
"Tôi" — an ordinary resident living inside the story (a tenant, a neighbour, a student, the guard, the maintenance worker). Calm, tired, precise, deadpan: never explains the joke or laughs at it. Knows numbers, floor levels and names. Enters early (`s1`–`s7`) with a personal stake or handicap under the rule.

## Story engine & three archetypes
One weird, topsy-turvy rule enters ordinary northern Vietnamese life and is treated with total seriousness. Pick **one** of three archetypes in `brief.md` (`## Archetype`) before outlining:

1. **`Personal Rule-Trap`** *(default for survival, sensory/physical anomalies, relational binds, and personal quirks)*:
   - An uncanny rule hits everyday life, locking the narrator (`Còn tôi?`) into a humiliating or disabling handicap by Act 1, then trapping them in a ticking **Double-Bind** in Act 2 that only a lateral hack from *inside* the rule's own physics can break.
   - Does **not** force ward committees, price boards, or neighbour tours when they would pad the middle into a catalogue.
2. **`Systemic Satire`** *(institutional/economic escalation)*:
   - An absurd rule bends markets, queues, prices, or ward committees. Every middle step must be driven by **active moves** from the narrator or a named rival (never a passive tour of neighbours), escalating until the system traps its own exploiters.
3. **`Hybrid VN Trap`** *(Vietnamese texture + ticking trap)*:
   - Combines authentic Vietnamese neighbourhood/family mechanics (building rules, alleys, markets, family duties) with a ticking Double-Bind and a lateral internal rule-hack.

## Premise test (all must be yes)
1. **Instant Cognitive Itch (`s1 ≤ 18` words):** Can the core rule be spoken in one plain sentence that makes a stranger immediately think *"Wait — how would anyone even live like that?"*
2. **Cannot-Turn-Off:** Is the trigger involuntary, ubiquitous in daily routines, or driven by an unavoidable need (never solvable by *"just stop doing X"*)?
3. **Surface-Loophole Blocker (`s2–s3`):** Does the setup immediately close the cheap cheat a viewer invents in the first 5 seconds?
4. **Drawable in One Frame (`sv-20`):** Can the rule's physical state or anomaly be drawn cleanly in flat 2D without crowds, vehicles in rapid motion, or photorealistic anatomy?
5. **Dual-Use / Weaponizable Flaw:** Can the rule's cost — or the narrator's embarrassing handicap — reverse polarity to become the sole mechanical key in Act 3?
6. **Archetype Fit:** Does it drive a ticking Double-Bind + lateral hack (`Personal Rule-Trap` / `Hybrid VN Trap`) or active rival/institutional escalation (`Systemic Satire`)?
7. **Original (`No copying`):** Not copied from any reference channel or earlier episode; `brief.md` names the closest reference and states how both the rule and the climax hack differ.

### Banned premise traps (auto-reject at G1)
- **Arbitrary bureaucratic counters:** Wrist/ankle/head tallies or step/word quotas that lack uncanny physical/perceptual curiosity.
- **Generic moral-to-element clichés:** Tears/lies/anger turning into rain/crystals/stone with no lateral mechanical hack or Double-Bind.
- **External enforcement monsters & magic-denial:** Outside monsters chasing rule-breakers, or endings that reveal *"it was just electricity / a dream / a road-widening project."*
- **"Birth name = superpower" / generic fantasy gifts:** Avoid tropes where names grant powers, or everyone gets cool superpowers with no paradoxical handicap.

## World and motifs
- Places (`sv-20` location bank): chung cư, ngõ, chợ, phường, tổ dân phố, nhà văn hoá, quán nước, trường học, gác xép, hầm gửi xe.
- People: bà tổ trưởng, ông bảo vệ, cán bộ phường, bà bán nước, thằng cu, ông cụ, cặp vợ chồng trẻ, anh shipper — named with a place (`"Bà Hạnh tầng năm"`, `"chú Toàn số nhà mười hai"`).
- Recurring Vietnamese texture (use only where it sharpens stakes, never as filler): official notices, red stamps, price boards, loudspeaker announcements, narrow alleys, shared courtyards.

## Tone limits
- Mock systems, habits, and literalized physics — never a real person, party, ethnicity, religion, disability, or real tragedy.
- No vulgarity, no sexual content beyond a hint (`"ngoại tình"` as a dry plot fact is the ceiling).
- No real brands, no real place names beyond generic ones (`"chung cư B5"`, `"phường"`).

## No copying
Study top channels such as *Rô Tới Chơi* and *In this town* (`@VoidCastRants`) for pacing, 5-Knot causality, deadpan tone, and lateral hacks — never reuse their premises, lines, characters, visual identity, or endings. Every brief's `## Not copying` must name the closest known episode and explain the difference in rule, mechanism, and climax hack.
