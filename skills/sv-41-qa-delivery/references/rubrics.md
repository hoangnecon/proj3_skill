# QA rubrics

Evidence is only evidence if you looked at it. Each review line names a file and says what you saw
(≥ 4 words, not "ok"). The gate checks that every evidence file is named and that the review is newer
than the evidence.

## Contact sheet (per shot, two frames at 25 % and 75 %)
1. Subject: is the thing the narration names at that moment the clearest thing in the frame?
2. Framing: shot size as planned; heads not cut by the top edge; no limb cut at a joint.
3. World edge: no empty paper/background showing at the sides (CA-05 catches most).
4. Captions band (y 1480–1760): no face, no important text, no overlay under it.
5. Consistency: characters look like their cast sheet (colours, hat, glasses); same set state as the
   previous shot unless a key changed it.
6. Life: the two frames differ (camera, pose, expression, key) unless the hold is intentional.
7. Text: every Vietnamese sign readable at phone size (≥ 22 px world at the shot's zoom); no typos,
   diacritics intact.
8. Overlays: ≤ 2, on the right word, bubble tail toward the speaker.

## Motion strips
See sv-21 strip checklist (walk, reach, sit/stand, ride, set key).

## Final watch (G7, at least once, full length, with sound)
| Check | Pass when |
|---|---|
| Sync | every cut lands on or just before its word; reach clicks on contact; key sounds on the change |
| Captions | match the voice, no line longer than 2 rows, no orphan single word |
| Rhythm | no stretch > 6 s without a visual change; the turn has its pause |
| Audio | voice clear everywhere; music never louder than the voice; no clicks, no sudden bed changes |
| Ending | button line holds ≥ 2.5 s, music resolves, no black frames at the end |
| Technical | 1080×1920, 30 fps, duration = voice + tail, file plays on a phone |

## Spot-check sheet (`qa/final_check.jpg`)
12 frames sampled from the encoded mp4. Compare against the contact sheet: same pictures, no
encoding artefacts (banding on halftone is acceptable at the delivery CRF, blocking is not), captions
present.
