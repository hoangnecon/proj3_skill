# sv-02 · Long-Form 16:9 Cinematic Production Standard (`v5.6.0`)

This skill extends the core `sv-00`–`sv-42` suite to **true cinematic 10–15 minute (`9.5–16.0 min`) 16:9 widescreen (`1920×1080`)** animated story videos for the `Nhím Là Lốp` audience, built and verified **Unit-by-Unit (`U1 → U2 → … → Uk`)** like standalone 2-minute episodes.

**Hard lesson from `v4.0.0` (`docs/rsi-forensics-v4.0-2026-11-22.md`) – `v5.6.0`:** Agents previously degraded quality on 10–15 minute movies by batch-dumping 160 shots at once across parallel workers, warping voice speed above `5.25 ups`, baking plot props/cat shadows into static backgrounds, leaking shrunken/normal sets or creatures across scenes, shrinking giant cats in close-ups instead of drawing partial anatomy (`giantCatPaw` / `giantCatHead`), freezing creatures like statues (`PR-09`), treadmill-running in place after stopping, or reviewing giant 55-shot slices at the end. **`v5.6.0` bans batch-dumping long-form shots:** every episode declares **`4–9` standalone Episodic Units (`U1..Uk`, `12–45` segments / `~2–2.5 min` / `~20–32` shots each, `SC-37`)** at the **pure `5.25 ups` Hải Đăng voice standard (`SC-35`, `SC-36`)**, and **each Unit `Uk` MUST be authored, linted, unit-QA rendered (`qa.mjs pass --unit Uk`), blind-reviewed (`blind_pack.mjs --unit Uk`), fixed, and sequentially gate-locked (`sv_gate.py pass-unit Uk`) BEFORE a single shot of `U(k+1)` is built!**

## 1. Opt-In & Long-Form Production Budgets (`G1`–`G5`)

A long-form 16:9 episode is an **explicit opt-in** (never the default 2–3 min Short):
- **`script.json`**: Set `"format": "16:9"`, `"length": "long_16x9"`, and top-level **`"units": [...]`** (`4–9` contiguous units `U1..Uk` with `id`, `title`, `seg_start`, `seg_end`, `scale_mode: "normal" | "tiny" | "mixed"`, `SC-37`).
- **`brief.md`**: Include `## Length override` with `user_quote: "<exact user instruction>"` (`SC-27`; never edit `standards/pacing.json` per episode) AND `## World & Tone` (or `## Thiết lập thế giới`) defining the everyday kid world, the 1-sentence 10-year-old physical rule, and the Cute / Spooky-Fun / Curious registers (`SC-32`).

## 2. 5-Chapter Narrative Depth, Pure `5.25 ups` Voice & Episodic `"units"` Plan (`G1`–`G3`)

Every chapter in `beats.md` MUST declare `### Chapter 1`..`### Chapter 5` (`Goal:`, `Obstacle:`, `Turn:`, `Mini-Cliffhanger:` / `Ending:`, `[sA-sB]` `10%–34%` span, `SC-29`), backed by `SC-30..37`:
1. **Linear Worldbuilding (`SC-32`) & Crystal-Clear Kid Hook (`SC-33`, `SC-36`):** Establish the everyday school/home world in `s1..s5` with an immediate curiosity cue in `s1..s3` (`?` or `tưởng tượng/chuyện gì/bất ngờ/bí mật/tí hon/thu nhỏ/khổng lồ`), spoken hook sentences `≤ 18` words, episode spoken sentences `≤ 24` words (`mean ≤ 15.5`, target `9–14`), and tactile kid terms (*"bập bênh bút chì"*, *"nút bấm màu xanh"*) instead of mechanical textbook jargon (`SC-36`) or flashback-rewind soup (`SC-32`).
2. **Emotional Triad & Live Kid Dialogue (`SC-34`):** `≥ 16%` (`≥ 20%` target) direct quoted kid banter (`“...”`) and `≥ 3` beats each of **Cute/Funny**, **Spooky-Fun Goosebumps**, and **Miniature/World Curiosity**.
3. **Pure `5.25 ups` Voice Integrity (`SC-35` & `sv-12`):** Never speed-warp VieNeu-TTS (`9.5–16 min` is natural!). Keep `90–100%` of segments at default `5.25 ups` (`wps` outside `[4.80, 5.45]` = ERROR, `> 10%` custom `wps` = WARN, `mean pause_after` in `[0.11, 0.32] s`).
4. **Mandatory Episodic `"units"` Plan (`SC-37`) & G2 Adversarial Story Doctor:** Partition `s1..sLast` contiguously into `4–9` standalone Episodic Units (`U1..Uk`, `12–45` segments / `~2–2.5 min` each, with `scale_mode: "normal" | "tiny" | "mixed"`), and pass `qa/story_doctor.json` (`score ≥ 80`, `meandering_stretches == []`) before G3 voice synthesis.

## 3. Cinematic 16:9 Visual Architecture, Scale-Mode Lock & Temporal Continuity (`G4` / `G5`)

1. **Spatial Location Packs, `scaleMode` & Stateless Plates (`24–30` Drawn Sets, `SE-15..20`, `SH-22`):**
   - Build **5–6 primary locations × 5 redrawn camera angles** (`wide`, `low`, 35–45° 3/4 `high` `SE-18`, `ots`/`reverse`, and `≥ 1` dedicated `closeup`/`pov` plate `actorScale: [0.95, 1.25]` per pack) sharing `landmarks` (`SE-17`) and varied botanical/prop silhouettes (`SE-19`), audited on `qa/design/overview_sets.jpg` and `qa/design/location_<loc>_pack.jpg`.
   - Declare **`SetDef.scaleMode: 'normal' | 'tiny'`** and **`stateless: true`** (`SE-20`): **NEVER** bake transient plot props, looming cat shadows, sticky tape, dropped glasses, or climax levers into static set backgrounds! **`SH-22`** blocks staging `'normal'` segments on `'tiny'` miniature sets or shrunken kids during `'tiny'` segments on `'normal'` sets.
2. **Quadruped Creature Anatomy, Temporal Guards & Cross-Angle Scale Lock (`PR-06`, `PR-08..09`, `SH-23`, `LA-03`):**
   - Register recurring creatures/vehicles in `SUBJECTS` with `≥ 4` poses and animated locomotion (`PR-06`, `PR-08`). Draw **true quadruped feline anatomy** (horizontal spine, articulated shoulder/haunch legs, paws, tail — never upright bowling-pin snowman `idle` blobs or 4-pipe `startled` legs!).
   - Declare **`firstSeg`**, **`appearSegs`**, and **`poseSegs`** on `SubjectDef` so **`SH-23`** blocks premature creature appearances (e.g. cat before its reveal segment) or stale stateful poses (e.g. sticky tape remaining after removal).
   - Declare **`scaleWithTinyActor: [minR, maxR]`** on giant creatures and **`partialOf: 'giantCat'`** on close-up partial anatomy subjects (`giantCatPaw`, `giantCatHead`). **`LA-03`** enforces per-shot `scaleWithTinyActor` bounds AND bans `> 1.85×` cross-angle creature scale spread across `tiny` sets — use `giantCatPaw` / `giantCatHead` (bleeding cleanly off frame edge per `PR-05`/`GE-19`) for tight close-ups instead of shrinking a giant creature!
   - **`PR-09`** bans frozen creature statues (`> 2.2 s` in 1 static pose at 1 spot without `path` locomotion or `≥ 2` distinct pose/expr beats).
3. **Distance-Locked Stop-Settle, Rear-Heel-Kick Runs & `3q`/`Front` Auto-Gait (`sv-21`, `MO-13..18`, `GE-20`):**
   - Side-view walks/runs lock `phase = dist / cyc` and drop `speed → 0` (`arms → 'down'`) at path stops (**zero treadmill running in place**), `footCycle` `run` drives a rear-peaked `42 px` heel-kick swing, and `Person3Q`/`Person` auto-gait along `path` (**zero ice-skating**), alongside 4-stage `jumpPhase` (`MO-17`), `+1200` elevated airspace (`GE-20`), `pushTarget` (`MO-13`), 3-layer straddle mounts (`MO-14`), and anti-freeze CI (`MO-18`, `PR-09`).
   - Keep `PR-01..06`, `PR-08..09`, `CH-01..10`, `TK-01..03`, `SE-01..20`, `CA-01..08`, `SH-01..23`, `LA-01..04`, `MO-01..18`, `GE-01`, `GE-04`, `GE-07`, `GE-10`, `GE-11`, `GE-15..20` at 0 errors.

## 4. Mandatory Episodic Unit-by-Unit Production Protocol (`U1 → U2 → … → Uk`, `G5`–`G7`)

**NEVER batch-author all `U1..Uk` shots at once or dispatch parallel shot-dumpers across unverified units!** After `G3` and `G4` pass, execute this exact sequential loop for **`k = 1 .. N`** (`U1`, then `U2`, ..., then `Uk`):

1. **Author ONLY Unit `Uk` (`~20–32` multi-scale shots covering `[unit.seg_start .. unit.seg_end]`):**
   - Use `node scripts/qa.mjs place <setId>` for collision-free `LA-01..04` coordinates, alternate `3.8–6.0 s` action/tracking takes (`camKeys` `CA-08`, `MO-16`) with `1.8–3.2 s` drawn `closeup`/`pov`/`ots` cuts every 2–3 wide shots (`SH-20`), advance across Location Packs (`SH-21`, `ShotDef.tr`), and run `node scripts/lint.mjs shots` + `node scripts/qa.mjs layout --unit Uk` until `0` errors on `Uk`. (`qa.mjs` supports progressive `SHOTS` authoring, automatically clamping `Uk`'s trailing shot to `U(k+1)`'s segment start!)
2. **Unit-Scoped QA Render & Blind Pack (`~20–30 s`, zero `$TMPDIR` bloat):**
   - Run `node scripts/qa.mjs pass --unit Uk` — renders **4-frame/shot `qa/units/Uk_contact.jpg`**, 8-frame `qa/shots/NN.jpg` (`Δ=%` pixel motion delta in `qa/motion_energy.json`), overlapping `qa/strips/*.jpg`, `qa/cards/NN.jpg`, HOT `qa/crops/NN.jpg`, runs `carry.py`, and runs `node scripts/blind_pack.mjs --unit Uk` → **`qa/blind_review/unit_Uk.json`** + updated `qa/blind_review/manifest.json`.
3. **Independent Blind Review & Fix Loop on `Uk`:**
   - Dispatch an independent Blind Reviewer (`reviewer != builder`) reading ONLY `qa/blind_review/unit_Uk.json`, `qa/units/Uk_contact.jpg`, `shot_strip`/`strips`, HOT `crops`, and `RUBRIC.md` (all 10 checks: `acting`, `contact`, `overlap`, `set`, `text`, `caption`, `beat`, `model`, `framing`, `variety`), submitting via `node scripts/qa.mjs review --batch`. Fix any `fix` shots in `Uk` and re-run `node scripts/qa.mjs pass --unit Uk` until every shot in `Uk` is `ok`.
4. **Sequential Unit Gate Lock (`sv_gate.py pass-unit Uk`):**
   - Write `gates/G5.Uk.review.md` (`verdict: pass`, no `TODO`, ≥ 4-word remark on `Uk_contact.jpg`, `mtime >= qa/units/Uk_contact.jpg`) and run **`python3 scripts/sv_gate.py pass-unit Uk`**.
   - `sv_gate.py` enforces **Strict Sequential Order** (`U2` FAILS unless `G5.U1` is already `"pass"` in `gates/log.jsonl`!), full segment coverage `[seg_start..seg_end]`, `0` lint errors on `Uk`, valid 10-check `blind_sha` reviews on every shot in `Uk`, and a `30 s` cooldown before logging `G5.Uk`. **Only after `G5.Uk` passes may work begin on `U(k+1)`!**
5. **Final Episode Consolidation, Foley Mix & Delivery (`G5` → `G6` → `G7`):**
   - Once all `G5.U1 .. G5.Uk` are locked (`python3 scripts/sv_gate.py status`), run `node scripts/qa.mjs pass changed` (builds episode-wide `qa/contact_p*.jpg` + `qa/contact_segments/seg_*.jpg` while `carry.py` preserves all unchanged unit reviews!), fill `gates/G5.review.md`, and run `python3 scripts/sv_gate.py pass G5` (which verifies every `G5.U1..G5.Uk` pass record + 0 global lint errors).
   - Run `python3 scripts/plan_audio.py` + `scripts/mix.py` (`G6`), then `SV_CHUNK_RENDER=1 node scripts/deliver.mjs` (`G7` → `out/final.mp4`), and verify suite health anytime with `python3 tools/validate_skills.py` & `python3 tools/benchmark_suite.py`.
