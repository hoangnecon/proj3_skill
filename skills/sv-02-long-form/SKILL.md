## 2. 5-Chapter Narrative Depth, 10-Year-Old Linear Storytelling & Brisk Voice Rhythm (`G1` / `G2` / `G3`)

Stretching a 3-minute premise thin across 10 minutes — or starting with a confusing flash-forward + `"Mọi chuyện bắt đầu ba phút trước"` rewind, stiff adult prose, dry `"cậu ta bảo..."` summary, and a flat `0.22 s`-pause drone — loses a 10-year-old viewer in under 60 seconds. Every chapter in `beats.md` MUST be structured explicitly with `### Chapter 1`..`### Chapter 5` (or `### Hồi 1`..`### Hồi 5`), each containing:
- **`Goal:`** (what the hero actively tries to achieve in THIS chapter)
- **`Obstacle:`** (the escalating rule twist, rival move, or environment threat)
- **`Turn:`** (the concrete reversal or discovery that changes the game)
- **`Mini-Cliffhanger:`** (for Ch 1–4; Ch 5 has **`Ending:`** — open/series-capable, `SC-24` zero preachy moral)
- **`[sA-sB]`** segment span (`10%–34%` of total segments per chapter, `SC-29`).

Required narrative & voice craft (`SC-29..35`):
1. **Clear Linear Worldbuilding (`SC-32`):** Establish the everyday relatable kid world (schoolroom, desk, home) + hero's personal quirk/handicap clearly in `s1..s5` (and document `## World & Tone` / `## Thiết lập thế giới` in `brief.md`), then trigger the 1-sentence physical rule linearly by `s6..s10` and slam the Surface-Loophole Blocker — **NEVER** open with a disorienting multi-location flash-forward followed by `"Mọi chuyện bắt đầu ba phút trước"`!
2. **10-Year-Old Readability & Tactile Physics (`SC-33`):** Keep every segment `≤ 26` words (`30` hard max) and every sentence `≤ 21` words (`mean ~ 8–10.5` words). Ban stiff adult clichés (`"bất khả xâm phạm"`, `"gạt bỏ hết hiềm khích"`, `"thế kẹt hai chiều"`); show clever rule-hacks through tactile school objects (pencil, eraser, chalk line, glowing marble, biscuit crumb), never textbook formulas.
3. **Emotional Triad & Live Quoted Kid Dialogue (`SC-34`):** Put live spoken kid banter in `≥ 20%` (`≥ 16%` floor) of segments using direct quotes (`“...”`) instead of dry `"cậu ta bảo..."` indirect reporting, and weave `≥ 3` beats each of **Cute/Funny** (playful banter, bug antenna bump, rumbling stomach), **Spooky-Fun Goosebumps** (creaking floor, glowing cat eyes in dark desk cavern, hushed `"Suỵt…"`), and **Miniature/World Curiosity** (ordinary school things transformed at 3cm).
4. **Brisk `5.65 ups` Anti-Drone Voice Rhythm (`SC-35` & `sv-12`):** `voice.py` automatically runs `long_16x9` at brisk **`5.65 ups`** (`sentence_gap_s: 0.12 s`, `default_pause_after_s: 0.14 s`, `overall_wps: [5.0, 6.2]`). Authors MUST keep `mean pause_after ≤ 0.20 s` (varying `0.10 s` rapid banter, `0.14 s` normal, `0.26–0.38 s` suspense turns) and explicitly modulate `wps` across `≥ 15%` (`≥ 12%` floor) of segments (`5.9–6.2` on chases/banter, `4.7–5.0` on spooky whispers/emotional turns).
5. **Escalating Rule Exploration (Ch 1–4) & Mandatory G2 Adversarial Story Doctor (`references/story-doctor-prompt.md`):** Every chapter exploits a **NEW consequence, tier, faction conflict, or physical edge case** (`≥ 12` causal Spine links `SC-31`, `≥ 3` distinct `Plant → Payoff` pairs `SC-30`). Dispatch a fresh independent agent to score `script.json` + `beats.md` into `qa/story_doctor.json` (`verdict == "pass"`, `score >= 80`, `meandering_stretches == []`) before synthesizing G3 voice.

## 3. Cinematic 16:9 Visual Architecture (`G4` / `G5`)

### 3.1 Spatial Location Packs, 3/4 Elevated Perspective & Silhouette Variety (`24–30` Drawn Sets, `SE-15`, `SE-17`, `SE-18` & `SE-19`)
Never trap a 10-minute movie inside 5 flat frontal walls, unbroken dollhouse wides (`SH-20`), single-location stalls (`SH-21`), or fake zoom-crops (`LA-03`). Organize every episode into **5–6 primary locations**, redrawing the **SAME physical space** from **5 distinct camera angles** per location (**target `24–30` drawn sets**, hard floor `≥ 15` ERROR, WARN `< 24`, WARN if `< 3` primary locations have `≥ 3` redrawn angles — `SE-15`). Every set in a Spatial Location Pack declares `location`, `shotScale`, `angle`, **`cameraDir`** (`'front' | 'reverse' | 'left' | 'right' | 'top' | 'low' | 'detail'`), and shared **`landmarks: string[]`**:
- **Establishing Wide (`angle: 'wide'`, `cameraDir: 'front'`):** Full 16:9 spatial geography (`bounds: [-320, -160, 2240, 1240]`, `plateCam: [960, 540, 1]`) establishing every shared landmark.
- **Low-Angle / Worm's-Eye (`angle: 'low'`, `cameraDir: 'low'`):** Camera near the floor looking UP (horizon low, towering furniture/giants/ceiling looming overhead — essential for scale awe!).
- **High-Angle / 3/4 Bird's-Eye (`angle: 'high'`, `cameraDir: 'front' | 'right' | 'left'`, `SE-18`):** Draw elevated overviews in **35–45° 3/4 perspective** with visible wall, desk, and fixture vertical height — **NEVER** pair a flat 90° board-game floorplan (`cameraDir: 'top'`) with horizon-rig `side`/`front` actors (`SE-18` Perspective-Horizon Consistency Guard warns)!
- **Side / Over-The-Shoulder / Reverse (`angle: 'ots'` or `'reverse'`, `cameraDir: 'left' | 'right' | 'reverse'`):** True redrawn counter-angle looking back toward the doorway, window, or opposing faction with matching room landmarks so conversations and chases cut cinematically instead of flat stage theater!
- **Dedicated Close-Up / Insert / POV (`angle: 'closeup'` or `'pov'`, `cameraDir: 'detail'`, `actorScale: [0.95, 1.25]`):** **Every primary Location Pack MUST include at least 1 dedicated `closeup` or `pov` plate** (`plan_assets.py` `v5.4.0+`) for hero reactions, hand-props, clocks, traps, or reveals — so G5 builders can alternate shot scales cleanly (`SH-20`, `CA-07`) without blurry `> 1.35×` wide-plate zooms (`LA-03`).
- **Continuity, Perspective & Anti-Clone-Stamp Guards (`SE-17`, `SE-18`, `SE-19`) & Location Strips:** `SE-17` validates `cameraDir` enum and `landmarks` array format (ERROR), bans 3+ sets in one location duplicating identical `(angle, shotScale, cameraDir)` (ERROR), and warns when a 3+ set location declares zero overlapping `landmarks`; `SE-18` warns when `cameraDir: 'top'` is paired with horizon-rig `side`/`front` actors; **`SE-19`** bans repeating `>= 9` verbatim identical curved SVG `<path>` silhouettes in one set (vary plant species — clover, daisy, curved grass blades, ferns, pebbles — scale, and angle). Always inspect every auto-tiled **`qa/design/location_<loc>_pack.jpg`** alongside location-sorted `qa/design/overview_sets.jpg` at G4!

### 3.2 4-View Character Rigs, `CartoonHand`, `Actor.lookAt`, Shot-Scale Alternation & Multi-Location Anime Cuts (`SH-20`, `SH-21`, `ShotDef.tr`, `MO-18`)
Use all 4 engine views (`Actor.view`: `'front' | 'side' | 'back' | '3q'`) plus foreground OTS framing (`Actor.fg: true`), subtle Dutch camera tilt (`ShotDef.tilt`), anime transitions (`ShotDef.tr`), and true cinema shot-scale & location alternation:
- **`view: 'back'` (`PersonBack`):** Full character seen from behind — ideal for **3rd-person OTS shots** (`fg: true`, `s: 1.5..2.0`, `x: 360` looking toward a boss/rival at `x: 1320`) and characters walking/gazing INTO the depth of the set!
- **`view: '3q'` (`Person3Q`):** Angled 3/4 perspective (`face: 1 | -1`) for natural diagonal two-shots, group confrontations across the `1920 px` width, and **automatic 3D `'3q'` stepped turnaround bridges (`|sx| >= 0.78` + `poseImpulse`, replacing `sx = 0.12` cardboard-pancake flips)**!
- **`view: 'side'` (`PersonSide`):** Planted-foot profile walks, climbs, 4-stage jumps (`jumpPhase`), sits, and reaches (`sv-21`).
- **`view: 'front'` (`Person`):** Direct reactions and comedic deadpan beats — capped at **`≤ 70%`** of actor placements (`SH-17`).
- **Living Micro-Acting, `CartoonHand`, `Actor.lookAt` & Anti-Freeze (`livingIdle`, `talkGesture`, `poseImpulse`, `MO-18`):** `Person`, `Person3Q`, and `PersonBack` continuously breathe, shift weight, micro-tilt heads, dart pupils (`livingIdle`), nod/gesture while speaking (`talkGesture`), pop on `p`/`e` transitions (`poseImpulse`), render articulated **`CartoonHand`** shapes (`'open' | 'point' | 'fist' | 'cup'` via `inferHandKind` so `pointL`/`pointR` extend real index fingers in `1.5×–1.7×` Close-Ups), and track 2D world eye-lines via **`Actor.lookAt`** (`[x, y]` or `[x, y, TT][]`); `SUBJECTS` run **`computeSubjectKinematics`** (aerial flight bob/bank + quadruped stride rock & paw `<DustPuff>` locked to straddle riders) and `poseU` spring-morph (`PR-08` enforces animated creature legs/wings/tail/breathing). **`MO-18`** errors when a `SUBJECT` in an attack pose (`pounce|charge|dive|attack`) freezes in place `> 1.8 s` without a lunge `path`/follow-through, and warns + marks HOT when an actor stands static `> 3.4 s` without acting/talk/path beats.
- **Anti-Dollhouse Shot-Scale Alternation (`SH-20`), Multi-Location Progression (`SH-21`) & Anime Cut Transitions (`ShotDef.tr`):** Never camp in distant full-stage wide/medium shots (`zoom <= 1.16`) or stall inside one Location Pack! **`SH-20`** flags `>= 3` (WARN) and `>= 4` (ERROR) consecutive wide/medium stage shots lacking close-up, POV, insert, or `fg` OTS breaks — alternate `3.8–6.0 s` establishing/medium action takes with `1.8–3.2 s` reaction `closeup`/`pov`/`ots` cuts. **`SH-21`** warns `> 11` and errors `> 16` unbroken shots in a single `location` when multiple Location Packs exist — cross-cut parallel spaces or drive momentum across rooms with **`ShotDef.tr`** (`'cut' | 'flash' | 'wipe' | 'whipL' | 'whipR' | 'iris'`, using `'whipL'`/`'whipR'` for kinetic cross-location cuts and `'iris'` for comic/miniature focus reveals)!
- **G4 Turnaround Verification:** `CastSheet` automatically renders **Front + 3/4 + Back** for every character look; inspect every angle before passing G4.

### 3.3 Giant Body-Part Continuity, Layout Grid, Airspace & Jump Physics (`PR-05..06`, `PR-08`, `GE-11`, `GE-19..20`, `LA-01..04`, `SH-18`, `MO-17`)
- Any giant partial body part (`thayHaiShoe`, giant leg, giant hand, giant cat paw) MUST be designed tall/wide enough (`≥ 720 px`, `PR-05`) and positioned so its severed top/side edge **exits cleanly past the frame boundary (`sy0 ≤ 24` or `sx0 ≤ 24` / `sx1 ≥ 1896`)** — NEVER floating amputated mid-air inside the frame (`GE-19` hard ERROR + HOT)!
